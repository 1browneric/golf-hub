# How to Use These Sessions

These are multisensory mental rehearsal scripts: you are not just picturing a shot, you are rehearsing it with sight, feel, sound, and breath, so that when the real moment arrives it feels like you have been there before. Listen slowly. Wherever the audio pauses, actually build the image before it continues.

**When to run them.** Evening, before bed: one full session, eyes closed, ten to twelve minutes, lights low. Session 1 first until the routine is automatic, then rotate Sessions 2 through 5. Commute: use only the Commute Version - eyes open, spoken self-talk, no closed-eye imagery while driving.

**Three rules that make it work.** First person, present tense - you are over the ball, not watching yourself on video. All senses - every rep includes something you see, something you feel, and something you hear. Rehearse success and recovery - most reps are pure execution, but a few include the nerves showing up and you running the routine anyway. That is what builds pressure-proofing.

Course details are limited to what is verified: the Posse is the original course with bluegrass fairways, rolling terrain, and lake views; the Outlaw has zoysia fairways, large greens, and a links-style layout with holes near the lake. Specific hole shapes come from your own memory on purpose - self-generated imagery is stronger than imagery someone else invents for you.
