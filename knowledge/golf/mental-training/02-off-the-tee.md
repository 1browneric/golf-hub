# Session 2 - Off the Tee at Paradise Pointe

Goal: own the first swing of the day on both courses, then build a menu of tee shots at different distances and shapes so no tee box feels unfamiliar.

## The Outlaw - first tee

It is a clear morning at Smithville Lake. You are standing on the first tee of the Outlaw. Feel the scene as you know it: the open, links-style look of the property, the tight zoysia turf underfoot, the breeze coming off the lake, the sound of carts and voices back by the clubhouse. Call up the first hole exactly as you remember it - where the trouble sits, where the wide side is, where a good drive finishes.

> Pause. Let the hole assemble from memory. The more detail, the better: tee markers, mow lines, the flag in the distance.

Notice your heart beating a little quicker. That is not fear. That is your body loading energy for you. Name it: I am ready.

Now the routine. Behind the ball. Driver in hand - feel the grip, the weight of the head. Read the wind off the lake. Pick one target on the wide side of the fairway, away from the trouble. Say it. That is my target.

See the flight: ball starts at your target, holds its line, lands in the short grass and chases out on the firm zoysia. Rehearsal swing - smooth, eighty percent feel. Walk in. One look. Exhale. Swing.

Hear the crack off the face. Feel the effortless speed. Watch it split your window. Hold the finish. First swing of the day, exactly how you drew it.

> Pause. Repeat this drive one more time, sharper.

## The Posse - first tee

Now shift to the Posse. The original course. Bluegrass fairways, softer underfoot than the Outlaw's zoysia. Rolling terrain, the lake showing between trees. Build the first tee as you know it - the angle of the hole, the slope of the land, where you want the ball to finish on that rolling ground.

> Pause. Assemble the hole from memory.

Same routine, no shortcuts. Decide. One target. See the full flight - account for the roll of the terrain, where the ball kicks after it lands. Feel the rehearsal swing. Walk in. One look. Go. Solid strike, ball riding your line, settling in the fairway. Accept it and smile - the round is open.

## The tee shot menu

Now run one rep of each shot below. Full routine every time, compressed to real speed. Change the hole picture for each - use real tee boxes from either course.

Driver, full send. Wide fairway, no trouble in play. Pick a distant target and let it release. Feel free speed, no steering.

Three wood, position. A shorter or tighter hole. The target is a spot in the fairway, not maximum distance. Feel the smoother tempo, flight a touch lower, ball finishing on your number for a full wedge in.

Hybrid or long iron off the tee. Trouble pinching the landing zone. See the lower, driving flight to a conservative target. This swing is about the middle of the face, nothing else.

Into the wind off the lake. Club up, swing easier. See the flatter flight boring through. Feel the three-quarter tempo. The wind is information, not a threat.

Trouble down one side. Tee up on the trouble side, aim at the fat side, and commit to the shape that moves the ball away from the hazard. Watch it start safe and stay safe.

> Pause after each rep. Finish the session with one more perfect Outlaw opening drive.
