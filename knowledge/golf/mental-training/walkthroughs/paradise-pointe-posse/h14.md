# Hole 14

Hole fourteen. Par 5, 585. Longest hole on this course and the number two handicap.

Water long and left of the green. Bunkers short and left. Two feet of fall, moving
left.

[BEAT — HOLD]

585, and every hazard is on the same side. That's a gift, because it gives the hole
one shape: lay back to a full number, favor the right coming in, never force the
second.

Driver leaves 310, which is nowhere near home. There's nothing to gain by pressing and
there's water to find by trying.

And know this before you're standing over the third: long and left is water, and the
only way you find it is a wedge that runs through the back. That's a club choice. Pick
the one that cannot get there.

Eyes closed. Driver.

[PAUSE]

310 left. Lay the second out to the right, to a number you can fly.

[PAUSE]

Then the wedge, right half, past that short sand. Short and right is the safe miss.

[PAUSE — LONG]

Under the hole, uphill, moving left. Right edge.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: everything that can hurt you is left or long. Right half, past the front
sand, and this is a five every time.
