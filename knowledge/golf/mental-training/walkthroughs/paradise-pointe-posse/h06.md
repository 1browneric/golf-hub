# Hole 6

Hole six. Par 5, 550, the number one handicap.

One bunker, left of the green. Two feet of slope, moving left. And a false front.

[BEAT — HOLD]

550 is a three-shot hole. Even a good drive leaves 275, and there's no version of this
where you get home in two.

Which puts the weight on the third shot, and that's the false front. The wedge has to
land on the surface, not the slope. So your second has one job: leave you a number you
trust to fly all the way on.

Eyes closed. Driver.

[PAUSE]

275 left. Second shot to your number, right-center. The bunker at the green is left,
so the miss coming in is right.

[PAUSE]

Then the wedge. Enough club to be past the front, at the right half.

[PAUSE — LONG]

Moving left. Right edge, and stay under the hole.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: the hardest hole here is decided by a wedge, not a driver. Leave yourself a
number you can fly onto the surface.
