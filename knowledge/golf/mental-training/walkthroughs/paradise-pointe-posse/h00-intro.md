# Before the first tee

Two things shape this course.

The greens are flat. Twelve of these eighteen come back with no tilt worth naming, so
for most of the round your eyes do all the work. I say so each time.

And the sand is everywhere. Seven of these greens are ringed — bunkered on every side.
On a ringed green there is no good miss and no clever side. There's the middle. You'll
hear middle a lot out here, and it's the whole strategy.

Wedges by loft — the 48, the 52, the 56, the 58.
