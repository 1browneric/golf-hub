# Hole 17

Hole seventeen. Par 4, 348, the 18 handicap. Easiest hole on the card.

Sand on every side of the green. Four feet of fall, back to front.

[BEAT]

Driver leaves 73 yards and a 58.

But four feet of fall changes the wedge and makes the pin matter. Get the sheet, then
land it at the front half and let the slope feed it. Above this hole is a putt you do not
need on the seventeenth.

Eyes closed. Driver.

[PAUSE]

73 in. The 58, front half, under the hole.

[PAUSE]

Uphill, and this green has four feet of fall in it. Take enough.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: four feet of fall on the easiest hole on the card. Below the hole or nothing.
