# Hole 8

Hole eight. Par 3, 141, the 15 handicap. Pitching wedge. Shortest hole on this course.

Ringed with sand — bunkers on every side. Two feet of fall, moving right.

[BEAT]

The shortest club into a green with sand on all four sides. A pitching wedge from 141
goes in the middle, and the middle is the whole target because there is nothing else.

Eyes closed. Pitching wedge, dead center.

[PAUSE]

Under it, moving right. Left edge, and enough pace.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: sand on every side and a pitching wedge in your hand. No bail-out, and from
141 you don't need one.
