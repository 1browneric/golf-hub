# Hole 18

Hole eighteen. Par 5, 560, the 4 handicap. Last one.

Bunkers ringing the green. Three feet of fall, moving left.

[BEAT — HOLD]

560 with sand on every side is a three-shot hole and there's no argument about it.
Driver leaves 285.

Which means the second shot has one job: leave you a wedge number you love. Not close to
it — the number.

That wedge is the last full shot of the round, it goes into sand on all four sides, and
there is no side to miss it on. The number is the whole defence, and the trap on this
hole is a second shot that gets greedy and leaves you a three-quarter.

Eyes closed. Driver.

[PAUSE]

285 left. Lay it to your full wedge number. Not close to it. Your number.

[PAUSE]

Then the wedge. Middle of the green, front half, under the hole.

[PAUSE — LONG]

From below, moving left. Right edge, and up the hill.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: 560 yards, and the only shot that matters is the third one. Lay up to a
number you'd bet on.
