# Hole 10

Hole ten. Par 3, 176, the 6 handicap. That's a 7 iron — and note the rating. The
hardest par 3 you'll play out here, and the sixth hardest hole on this card.

One bunker, left. Two feet of slope, moving right. And the left edge runs off.

[BEAT]

Sand left, run-off left. Everything says right of center, and for once the slope agrees
because the surface leans that way anyhow. Right is the miss.

Eyes closed. 7 iron, center-right.

[PAUSE]

Moving right, and more than looks correct. Left edge, and stay below the hole.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: the sixth hardest hole on this card is a 7 iron. Right of center, and a three
is a good score.
