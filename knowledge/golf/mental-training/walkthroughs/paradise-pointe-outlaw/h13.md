# Hole 13 - Paradise Pointe · Outlaw

You are on the thirteenth tee. Par 4, 434 yards from the Blue tees. Feel tight zoysia underfoot and the breeze coming off the lake.

Play it backward. You want a mid iron into the left side of the green, so the tee shot only has to put you on that number. Nothing more.

Here is the one place the ball cannot go: bunker right of the green. So the target is the left side of the green. Aim there and a normal miss is still grass.

Now the routine, compressed. Decide, behind the ball. One target, not a zone. See the full flight, launch to landing. Feel one rehearsal swing at that tempo. Walk in, one look, and go. Then accept it, whatever it does.

> Pause.

Now the approach. The pin does not matter yet, the left side of the green does. Same routine, committed swing, ball finishing right there.

The green: Lidar reads this green nearly flat — no major slope; trust your eyes on the ground.

Draw the ghost circle, three feet around the hole. Read it from the low side. Lag it dead, dying inside the circle.

Hear the ball drop into the cup. One breath. Walk to the next tee with nothing carried over.
