# Hole 3

Hole three. Par 3, 159, the 15 handicap. 9 iron.

Sand left and right. This is the first of the two greens that run away from you — three
feet, front to back — and the left side falls away.

[BEAT]

Anything flown to the back releases toward the collar. Land it on the front portion and
let the slope take it. Sand both sides, no good miss.

Eyes closed. 9 iron, lower and flatter than 159 asks for. Front third, center-right, off
that left edge.

[PAUSE]

From the front third of a green that falls away you're above most pins, putting down the
slope. Die it at the edge.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: front third of the green and let it feed. The back of this green is not a
target, it's a hazard.
