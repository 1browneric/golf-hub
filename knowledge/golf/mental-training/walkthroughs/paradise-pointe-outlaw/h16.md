# Hole 16 - Paradise Pointe · Outlaw

You are on the sixteenth tee. Par 3, 139 yards from the Blue tees. Feel tight zoysia underfoot and the breeze coming off the lake.

Play it backward. There is one shot here and it is this one, so the plan is simply the right side of the green.

Here is the one place the ball cannot go: bunkers left and short of the green. So the target is the right side of the green. Aim there and a normal miss is still grass.

Now the routine, compressed. Decide, behind the ball. One target, not a zone. See the full flight, launch to landing. Feel one rehearsal swing at that tempo. Walk in, one look, and go. Then accept it, whatever it does.

> Pause.

Watch the ball land on the right side of the green, take its hop, and settle.

The green: General slope back-to-front, toward the left (~3 ft, from lidar) — big picture only, not the subtle break. Falls away left — bail anywhere but there.

Draw the ghost circle, three feet around the hole. Read it from the low side. Lag it dead, dying inside the circle.

Hear the ball drop into the cup. One breath. Walk to the next tee with nothing carried over.
