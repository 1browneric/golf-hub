# Hole 16

Hole sixteen. Par 3, 139, the 16 handicap. Pitching wedge. Shortest hole on this
course.

Sand short of the green and left of it. Three feet of fall, moving left. And the left
side falls away.

[BEAT]

Everything to avoid is left — the sand, the lean, the run-off — and the bunker short means
it has to carry. Right half, past that bunker, and right is the miss.

Eyes closed. Pitching wedge, over that short sand, right half.

[PAUSE]

Below the hole, moving left. Right edge, and don't die it.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: sand, slope and run-off, all of it left. Right half of the green, and this is
a birdie hole.
