# Hole 4

Hole four. Par 4, 327, the 17 handicap. Second easiest on the card.

Sand short of the green and left of it. Three feet of fall. And the right side falls
away.

[BEAT]

Short enough to turn it loose. Driver leaves 52 yards and a 58. The wedge is the whole
hole: sand short, sand left, a right edge that runs off. That leaves the middle, and
the middle is plenty from 52 yards.

Eyes closed. Driver.

[PAUSE]

52 in. The 58, dead center, past the short bunker, nowhere near either edge.

[PAUSE]

Under it, straight uphill. Take enough.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: 52 yards, and the only target is the middle. Short is sand, left is sand,
right is gone.
