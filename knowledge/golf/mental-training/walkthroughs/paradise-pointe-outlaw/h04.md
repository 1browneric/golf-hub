# Hole 4 - Paradise Pointe · Outlaw

You are on the fourth tee. Par 4, 327 yards from the Blue tees. Feel tight zoysia underfoot and the breeze coming off the lake.

Play it backward. You want a stock 58 degree - do not walk it inside 90 yards, so the tee shot only has to put you on that number. Nothing more.

Here is the one place the ball cannot go: bunkers left and short of the green. So the target is the right side of the green. Aim there and a normal miss is still grass.

Now the routine, compressed. Decide, behind the ball. One target, not a zone. See the full flight, launch to landing. Feel one rehearsal swing at that tempo. Walk in, one look, and go. Then accept it, whatever it does.

> Pause.

Now the approach. The pin does not matter yet, the right side of the green does. Same routine, committed swing, ball finishing right there.

The green: General slope back-to-front (~3 ft, from lidar) — big picture only, not the subtle break. Falls away right — bail anywhere but there.

You have the wedge you planned for. Pick your landing spot, a patch the size of a towel, and see the ball land on it, hop once, and trickle to tap-in range.

Hear the ball drop into the cup. One breath. Walk to the next tee with nothing carried over.
