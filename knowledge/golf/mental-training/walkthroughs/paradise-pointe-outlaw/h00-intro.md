# Before the first tee

One thing to carry all day: the edges of these greens are not safe.

Eleven of these eighteen have a side that falls away or a front that sits up and rejects
a short shot. A ball on the edge of a green out here often isn't on the green — it's in
the collar, down a bank, or back at your feet.

So the instruction on this course will sound conservative. Aiming at the middle of an
Outlaw green is the only way to be putting.

And two of these greens run away from you, front to back. You'll know both when you
get there.

Wedges by loft — the 48, the 52, the 56, the 58.
