# Hole 14

Hole fourteen. Par 4, 352, the 12 handicap.

One bunker, left of the green. Three feet of fall. And nothing else — no run-off, no
false front.

[BEAT]

352 with one hazard on it. Mini driver leaves 112 and a 52. Driver leaves 77 and a 58.
Nothing on this green punishes the shorter club, so hit the driver.

Eyes closed. Driver.

[PAUSE]

77 in. The 58 sitting down right of the flag, away from the sand left.

[PAUSE]

Uphill, and you're under it. Go at it.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: one bunker, left, and nothing else on this green. Miss it right and you are
still putting — that is rare out here.
