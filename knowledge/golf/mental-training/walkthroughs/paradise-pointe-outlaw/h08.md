# Hole 8

Hole eight. Par 4, 356, the 11 handicap. The strangest green on the card.

Sand short and left of the green. This is the second runaway green, and it has a false
front as well: it rejects a short shot, and then the surface runs away from you, front
to back, two feet, moving left.

[BEAT — HOLD]

The front edge won't let a short one on, and the moment you are on, the ground runs
downhill toward the back.

So the shot is exactly this: enough club to clear the front and no more. Everything
releases once it lands, which means the club that only just gets there is the club that
finishes closest.

Mini driver leaves 116 and a 52 — a controllable full swing, which is what this green
wants. Off the tee, right-center, because the sand is short and left.

Eyes closed. Mini driver, right-center.

[PAUSE]

116 in. The 52, right half, landing just past the front edge and expecting it to
release.

[PAUSE]

Unless the pin is at the very front you are above the hole, going down the slope, and
it moves left. Right edge, dying.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: enough club to get on, and not one yard more. This green punishes both
misses, in opposite directions.
