# Hole 15 - Paradise Pointe · Outlaw

You are on the fifteenth tee. Par 4, 402 yards from the Blue tees. Feel tight zoysia underfoot and the breeze coming off the lake.

Play it backward. You want a mid iron into the back half of the green, so the tee shot only has to put you on that number. Nothing more.

Here is the one place the ball cannot go: bunkers left, right and short of the green. So the target is the back half of the green. Aim there and a normal miss is still grass.

Now the routine, compressed. Decide, behind the ball. One target, not a zone. See the full flight, launch to landing. Feel one rehearsal swing at that tempo. Walk in, one look, and go. Then accept it, whatever it does.

> Pause.

Now the approach. The pin does not matter yet, the back half of the green does. Same routine, committed swing, ball finishing right there.

The green: General slope back-to-front (~3 ft, from lidar) — big picture only, not the subtle break.

Draw the ghost circle, three feet around the hole. Read it from the low side. Lag it dead, dying inside the circle.

Hear the ball drop into the cup. One breath. Walk to the next tee with nothing carried over.
