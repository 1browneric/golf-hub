# Hole 1 - Paradise Pointe · Outlaw

You are on the first tee. Par 4, 374 yards from the Blue tees. Feel tight zoysia underfoot and the breeze coming off the lake.

Play it backward. You want a full 48 degree from about 127 yards, so the tee shot only has to put you on that number. Nothing more.

Here is the one place the ball cannot go: penalty area down the left off the tee; bunker right of the green. So the target is the right side of the fairway. Aim there and a normal miss is still grass.

Now the routine, compressed. Decide, behind the ball. One target, not a zone. See the full flight, launch to landing. Feel one rehearsal swing at that tempo. Walk in, one look, and go. Then accept it, whatever it does.

> Pause.

Now the approach. The pin does not matter yet, the fat side of the green does. Same routine, committed swing, ball finishing right there.

The green: General slope back-to-front, toward the left (~3 ft, from lidar) — big picture only, not the subtle break.

You have the wedge you planned for. Pick your landing spot, a patch the size of a towel, and see the ball land on it, hop once, and trickle to tap-in range.

Hear the ball drop into the cup. One breath. Walk to the next tee with nothing carried over.
