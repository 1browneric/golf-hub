# Hole 1

Hole one. Par 4, 374, the 7 handicap.

Water left. One bunker, right of the green. Three feet of fall, moving left.

[BEAT]

The hole asks two opposite things: away from the water going out, away from the sand
coming in. Driver leaves 99 and a 56, as long as the start line is off the water.

Now play it, eyes closed, first person.

Driver, aimed off the water. Ordinary swing, unusual start line.

[PAUSE]

Short grass. 99 in. The 56 at the left half, away from the bunker. Left is the miss.

[PAUSE]

Below the hole, uphill, moving left. Right edge.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: water left off the tee, sand right at the green. Two different aims on one
hole — don't carry the first one into the second.
