# Hole 2

Hole two. Par 4, 415, and the number three handicap.

Sand short of the green and right of it. Two feet of fall.

[BEAT — HOLD]

415 on the third hardest hole means you take par and walk. Nothing about this hole rewards
a birdie attempt: sand short and sand right, and the only clean ground is long and left.

Mini driver leaves 175 and an 8 iron. Driver leaves 140 and a pitching wedge. Take the
mini driver, and the reason is the bunker short: a pitching wedge from 140 into a
bunker-fronted green is the club people steer and leave in the sand. The 8 iron doesn't
ask you to be exact.

Eyes closed. Mini driver.

[PAUSE]

175 left. The 8 iron, left half, off the sand right, and enough of it to clear the
short bunker. Long and left is the free ground.

[PAUSE]

Uphill, from underneath it. Go at it.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: sand short and sand right. The club that clears both is the one to hit. Take
the 8 iron.
