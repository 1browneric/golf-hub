# Hole 5

Hole five. Par 5, 549, the number one handicap.

Water down the right side. One bunker, short of the green. Three feet of fall, moving
right — and the right side falls away.

[BEAT — HOLD]

Everything dangerous is right. Water right off the tee. Right lean on the green. Right
edge running off. Three features, one side, and it all plays left.

549 is a three-shot hole. Driver leaves 274, which isn't a number you go at. So your
second puts a full wedge in your hand, and that wedge has one requirement: it carries
the bunker sitting short.

Eyes closed. Driver, started away from the water.

[PAUSE]

274 left. Second shot to your number, left side of the fairway.

[PAUSE]

Then the wedge, past the short bunker, at the left half. Long and left is the miss.

[PAUSE — LONG]

Moving right, and more than you read. Left edge, and never above it on the right.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: three hazards, one side. Every shot on the number one handicap starts left of
where you think it should.
