# Hole 18

Hole eighteen. Par 4, 349 — and the card rates it the 4 handicap.

One bunker, short of the green. Three feet of fall.

[BEAT — HOLD]

349 is the third shortest par 4 out here and they've rated it fourth hardest. It isn't
distance and it isn't the green — one bunker, and it sits short.

So the whole hole is that bunker: every approach has to carry it, however short the
club. That's where a 349-yard hole takes strokes — people wedging it into the sand from
inside a hundred.

Driver leaves 74 yards and a 58. Mini driver leaves 109 and a 52. Hit the driver — from
74 the 58 comes down steeper and stops sooner, and stopping it is the point on a green
with three feet of fall.

Eyes closed. Driver. Last routine of the day.

[PAUSE]

74 in. The 58, past the bunker, middle of the green. Long is clean ground.

[PAUSE]

Straight uphill. Take enough, and walk it in.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: 349 yards, rated fourth hardest. Believe the card, carry the bunker, and
finish.
