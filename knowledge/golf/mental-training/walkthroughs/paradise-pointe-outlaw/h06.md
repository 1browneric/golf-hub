# Hole 6

Hole six. Par 3, 161, the 13 handicap. 8 iron.

One bunker, right. Three feet of fall. And the right side of this green falls away.

[BEAT]

Two reasons to be left of center and both are on the right: the sand and the run-off.
Start it at the middle. If it drifts left, left is grass. It cannot drift right.

Eyes closed. 8 iron, center-left.

[PAUSE]

Below the hole, straight uphill. Take enough.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: this green forgives you left and it does not forgive you right. Aim
accordingly.
