# Hole 5

Hole five. Par 3, 209 yards, the 11 handicap. That's a 4 iron.

One bunker, right. Three feet of fall, back to front. And a false front — the first of
the day.

[BEAT]

209 over a false front. You cannot be short. Carry it to the middle and take three.
Left is the miss.

Eyes closed. 4 iron, high, past that front edge, left half.

[PAUSE]

Uphill from below the hole. Take enough.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: 209 is a long way to fly a false front. Nothing on this hole is worse than
being short of it.
