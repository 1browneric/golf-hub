# Hole 8

Hole eight. Par 4, 387, the 5 handicap. Fifth hardest, and it doesn't look it.

Sand left of the green. Three feet of fall, back to front. And a false front.

[BEAT]

Driver leaves 112 and a 52. The number is the easy part; the front edge is the problem,
because a 52 that lands on it does not stay there. Right-center off the tee — the sand is
left and so is the miss.

Eyes closed. Driver, right-center.

[PAUSE]

112 in. The 52 aimed right of the pin, with enough on it to finish past the front.

[PAUSE]

Middle of the green, under the flag, uphill. Take enough.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: the 5 handicap hands you a 52 from the fairway and then asks you to fly it
all the way on. Take the extra half club.
