# Hole 6

Hole six. Par 4, 330, the 17 handicap. Easiest hole on this nine.

One bunker, right of the green. Four feet of fall, and the green moves the ball
right — as steep as any green out here.

[BEAT]

Driver leaves 55 and a 58. Four feet of fall is the only thought: under the hole. Get the
pin — a back pin puts you above it whatever you do.

Eyes closed. Driver.

[PAUSE]

55 in. The 58 landing left of the flag and under it.

[PAUSE]

Uphill, moving right. Left edge, and enough pace.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: four feet of fall turns a putt from above into a three at 330 yards. Stay
under it.
