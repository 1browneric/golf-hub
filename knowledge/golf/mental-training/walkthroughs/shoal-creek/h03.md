# Hole 3 - Shoal Creek Golf Course

You are on the third tee. Par 3, 168 yards from the Blue tees. Feel the turf underfoot and the air on your face.

Play it backward. There is one shot here and it is this one, so the plan is simply the middle of the green.

Here is the one place the ball cannot go: bunkers left and right of the green. So the target is the middle of the green. Aim there and a normal miss is still grass.

Now the routine, compressed. Decide, behind the ball. One target, not a zone. See the full flight, launch to landing. Feel one rehearsal swing at that tempo. Walk in, one look, and go. Then accept it, whatever it does.

> Pause.

Watch the ball land on the middle of the green, take its hop, and settle.

The green: Lidar reads this green nearly flat — no major slope; trust your eyes on the ground. Falls away right — bail anywhere but there.

Draw the ghost circle, three feet around the hole. Read it from the low side. Lag it dead, dying inside the circle.

Hear the ball drop into the cup. One breath. Walk to the next tee with nothing carried over.
