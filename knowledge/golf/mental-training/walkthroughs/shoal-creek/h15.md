# Hole 15

Hole fifteen. Par 4, 387, the 8 handicap.

Water short and left of the green. Bunker left as well. Three feet of fall, moving
right.  And a false front.

[BEAT]

Every feature says the same thing: not short, not left. Driver leaves 112 and a 52, and
that 52 has to carry the front edge, because short here isn't a chip. It's a drop. Long
and right is the free ground.

Eyes closed. Driver, right side, away from the water.

[PAUSE]

112 in. The 52 aimed right of the pin. Between clubs, the longer one.

[PAUSE]

Below the hole, uphill, moving right. Left edge.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: short of this green is a penalty stroke, and nothing else on it is. That's
the whole hole in one sentence.
