# Hole 15 - Shoal Creek Golf Course

You are on the fifteenth tee. Par 4, 387 yards from the Blue tees. Feel the turf underfoot and the air on your face.

Play it backward. You want a full pitching wedge from about 138 yards, so the tee shot only has to put you on that number. Nothing more.

Here is the one place the ball cannot go: water short of the green; water short-left of the green; bunker left of the green. So the target is the back half of the green. Aim there and a normal miss is still grass.

Now the routine, compressed. Decide, behind the ball. One target, not a zone. See the full flight, launch to landing. Feel one rehearsal swing at that tempo. Walk in, one look, and go. Then accept it, whatever it does.

> Pause.

Now the approach. The pin does not matter yet, the back half of the green does. Same routine, committed swing, ball finishing right there.

The green: General slope back-to-front, toward the right (~3 ft, from lidar) — big picture only, not the subtle break. False front — the green sits up; anything short spins off. Carry it to the middle.

You have the wedge you planned for. Pick your landing spot, a patch the size of a towel, and see the ball land on it, hop once, and trickle to tap-in range.

Hear the ball drop into the cup. One breath. Walk to the next tee with nothing carried over.
