# Before the first tee

Two things about this place.

Every odd handicap on the card is on the front nine — all nine of them, the number
one and the number three included. The first hole you play is the number three.

And seven of these greens sit up on a false front. Short of the surface comes back to
you. I name it every time.

Wedges by loft — the 48, the 52, the 56, the 58.
