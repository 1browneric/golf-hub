# Hole 16

Hole sixteen. Par 4, 318, the 18 handicap. Shortest par 4 on the card and the easiest
hole on it, with one thing that can ruin the number.

Water crosses the fairway. Bunker left of the green. Two feet of fall, and the left
edge runs off.

[BEAT — HOLD]

The carry over that water isn't in here. Laser it or pace it before you pull a club,
every time, because it moves with the markers.

Once you have it: driver leaves 43 yards if it's clear. 3 hybrid leaves 98 and takes
most of the risk out. Either way the green wants the same thing — sand left, run-off
left, so everything works from the right.

Eyes closed. Say the number came back and it says driver. Driver, and it carries.

[PAUSE]

Dry, and 43 in. The wedge at the right half, never at the left edge.

[PAUSE]

Uphill from under the hole. Take enough.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: the 18 handicap is only the 18 handicap after you know the carry. Get the
number, then pick the club.
