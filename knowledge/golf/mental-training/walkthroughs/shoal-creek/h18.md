# Hole 18

Hole eighteen. Par 4, 414, the number two handicap.

One bunker, left of the green. The surface itself is flat — the front is not. False
front, and short comes back.

[BEAT — HOLD]

Mini driver leaves 174 and an 8 iron. Driver leaves 139 and a pitching wedge. Take the
mini driver, and the reason is the front edge, not the fairway: an 8 iron carries it, and
a pitching wedge is the club people leave on the slope.

414 as the number two handicap means par is a win. The sand is left, the miss is
right, and the mini driver version of this hole has no bad outcome in it.

Eyes closed. Mini driver. Last full swing of consequence.

[PAUSE]

Fairway. 174 in. The 8 iron, right half, past the front, onto the middle.

[PAUSE]

Flat surface. Pick the line and hit it.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: 414, the number two handicap, and the club that solves it is an 8 iron.
Mini driver, 8 iron, done.
