# Hole 9

Hole nine. Par 5, 494, the 9 handicap. Reachable.

Sand both sides of the green. Three feet of slope toward the left, and the left side
falls away.

[BEAT — HOLD]

Hear the green first, because it's the reason for the rating. Three feet of tilt toward
the left, sand both sides, a left edge that doesn't hold. Everything this green does, it
does toward the one place you cannot be. Above the hole and left is dead.

Driver leaves 219, and 219 is a 3 hybrid. So the decision in the fairway is a hybrid
at that green or a lay-up to a full wedge from a hundred. Both are good golf. Missing
left is not.

Eyes closed. Driver.

[PAUSE]

Fairway, 219 left. Say you go — 3 hybrid at the right half.

[PAUSE]

Moving left, and more than you read. Right edge.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: right of the middle on this green, whatever club you get there with. Left
is how this hole becomes a seven.
