# Hole 7

Hole seven. Par 5, 543, and it's the number one handicap.

One bunker, right of the green. Four feet of fall, back to front, moving right.

[BEAT — HOLD]

543 is a three-shot hole, and the number one handicap earns that rating with length,
not trouble. There's one bunker out there. Which tells you how it beats people: it
tempts them into a shot they don't have.

Even a good drive leaves 268. Your second has one job and it isn't distance — it's to put
a full wedge number in your hand. Not a half, not a three-quarter. And get the pin: four
feet of fall means a back flag is above you wherever you land it.

Eyes closed. Driver.

[PAUSE]

Fairway. 268 left. Lay it to your number, left-center. The sand at the green is
right, so the miss coming in is left.

[PAUSE]

The wedge from your number. Left half, under the flag.

[PAUSE — LONG]

Below the hole, uphill, moving right. Left edge, and get it to the hole.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: the number one handicap has one bunker on it. It beats you at 268 yards,
not at the green. Lay up to a number you love.
