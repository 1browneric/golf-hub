# Hole 14

Hole fourteen. Par 4, 383, the 6 handicap.

Sand right of the green. Three feet of fall, moving left. And the left edge falls
away.

[BEAT]

Both sides cost you. Driver leaves 108 and a 56. Mini driver leaves 143 and a pitching
wedge. Take the driver — the target is the gap between sand and run-off and you hold it
better with a 56.

Eyes closed. Driver.

[PAUSE]

108 in. The 56 left of the sand, yes — but left of the flag, not left of the green.
Over that left edge it's gone.

[PAUSE]

Under the hole, moving left. Right edge, a touch of it.

[PAUSE]

[BEAT — RUN IT AGAIN]

The call: the gap between the sand and the run-off is the entire target, and there is
no miss outside it.
