# Session 5 - Playing Three Holes

Goal: stitch everything together - routine, tee shot, wedge, lag putt, short putt - inside real holes with real strategy. Use three consecutive holes you know well from either course. The strategy rules are conservative target, committed swing: aim away from trouble, favor the fat side of greens, never bring double bogey into play on purpose.

## Hole one - the opener

Back on the first tee. Build the hole from memory. Before you pull a club, play the hole backward in your mind: where do you want the approach from, and what tee shot leaves that spot with the least risk? That is your plan. Now execute it - full routine, committed swing, ball finishing in position.

> Pause. Tee shot complete.

Approach: the pin does not matter yet - the fat side of the green does. Pick the target that misses safe. Routine. Flight. Strike. Ball on the green, thirty feet away. Draw the ghost circle, read from the low side, lag it dead inside two feet. Tap in. Walk off with a stress-free par and feel what that feels like - boring golf is winning golf.

## Hole two - trouble in play

Second hole, as you know it. This time there is real trouble - water, trees, or a penalty area on one side. The strategy rule: identify the one place you absolutely cannot go, aim far enough away that a normal miss still finds grass, and commit fully to that conservative target. A committed swing at a safe target beats a scared swing at a perfect one every time.

> Pause. Play the tee shot.

Now your approach leaves you a wedge inside fifty - use the rough-lie or short-sided rep, whichever fits the hole in your mind. Land the towel. Then hole the six-footer: read, rehearsal, one look, rattle. That is a par saved by a wedge and a putt - the exact skills you are training.

## Hole three - pressure finish

Third hole. Add stakes: you are one under your target score with this hole to play. Feel the pull to protect it - the tight grip, the quick tempo trying to sneak in. Notice it. Name it. Then hand control back to the routine. The routine does not know the score.

Tee shot to the wide side. Approach to the middle of the green. Two putts from twenty-five feet - lag inside the circle, tap in. Walk off the green having executed under pressure, and let the feeling settle in: you have been here before. Because now you have.

> Pause. Slow breath. End the session on that image.
