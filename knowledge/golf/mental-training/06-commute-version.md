# Commute Version - Eyes Open, Five Minutes

For the drive. This is spoken self-talk, not deep visualization: eyes on the road, no relaxation sequence, no closed-eye imagery. It is a verbal walkthrough - the same way a caddie would talk you through tomorrow's shots.

Say it out loud or silently, in first person, present tense. One routine recital plus three shots. Total about five minutes.

One - Recite the routine: decide, one target, see it, feel it, walk in and go, accept. Say all six steps once.

Two - Talk through one tee shot: name the course, the hole, the target, the shape, and the result. Example: Outlaw one, driver, wide side at the far mow line, ball rides the line, short grass, position A.

Three - Talk through one wedge: the lie, the towel, the flight, the finish. Example: forty yards, fairway, towel short of the middle pin, one hop, three feet, tap-in.

Four - Talk through one putt and finish with the sound: the read, the pace, the last turn of the ball, the rattle in the cup.

That is the whole commute session. Save the full sessions for the evening, when your eyes can close and the imagery can go deep.

**Weekly rotation.** Session 1 every night for the first three or four nights until the routine is automatic. Then rotate - tee shots one night, wedges the next, putting the next, and the three-hole strategy round the night before you play. Commute version any day you drive. Mental reps compound the same way range reps do: small, consistent, daily.
