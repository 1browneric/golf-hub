## 1. No double bogeys.

### Why

Doubles come from playing for birdie where the hole asked for par or bogey. The
count reads your strategy, not your ball striking.

### What to do

Eliminate doubles rather than force birdies. In strokes gained a double is 6x
worse than a bogey.

## 2. No bogeys on par 5s.

### Why

Aggression dressed up as opportunity. It follows a bad tee shot or layup on a
hole you had already called a birdie hole.

### What to do

Get as close to the green as you can, without taking on a hazard and bringing
penalty shots into it.

## 3. No three-putts.

### Why

Speed, not read. The ones that hurt come from 20-40 ft, trying to make the first
one and running it 5 ft past.

### What to do

Practice speed uphill and downhill. 50% of your putting time on speed, 25% on
5-15 ft, 25% simulating a putting course.

## 4. No bogeys with 9 iron or less.

### Why

150 yds and in. A bogey from your scoring clubs means the target was wrong, not
the swing.

### What to do

Stop forcing birdies. From 79 to 76, 82% of the improvement is bogeys avoided,
not birdies made.

## 5. No double chips.

### Why

A blown easy save is forcing something the spot did not allow. In trouble, the
bogey is the good outcome.

### What to do

Aim for a 1:1 ratio. Further from the green than the pin is, get it on the
surface safely.

## 4 or fewer.

Not perfect golf, fewer disasters.
